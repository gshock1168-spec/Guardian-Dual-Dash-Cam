Privacy and local-device data statement.

Guardian Dual Dash Cam is designed so user recordings and trip details stay on the user's device.

We do not collect user data

Guardian does not collect, sell, or transfer user recordings, location history, audio, telemetry, or personal driving data to our servers. Video clips, optional audio, and travel overlays remain stored locally on the user’s device unless the user chooses to share a clip.

User-controlled sharing

When a user shares a clip, the user chooses where it goes, such as an insurance agent, law enforcement contact, email, text message, or another app on the device.

Storage management

Users can manage saved clips and set clip-retention limits. Older clips may be automatically deleted according to the user’s settings to help preserve device storage.

No advertising profile

The app is not designed to build advertising profiles from driving activity, recordings, contacts, or location data.
