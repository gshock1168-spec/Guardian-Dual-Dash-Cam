App permissions and why they are needed.

Guardian only asks for permissions needed to operate as a mobile dash cam.

Camera

Required to record the road view and, on supported devices, the interior cabin view. Without camera access, Guardian cannot function as a dash cam.

Microphone

Used only when the user turns on optional audio recording. This can help document conversations, sounds, or other incident-related context. Audio recording can be disabled.

Location

Used to show trip context such as speed, street information, and location-based telemetry overlays. This helps make saved clips more useful for insurance claims, law enforcement reports, and personal documentation.

Storage / media access

Used to save, display, manage, delete, and share dash cam clips on the device. Guardian uses loop recording controls to help manage storage.

Notifications / foreground service

Used so recording can continue reliably while the app is active, in the background, or while the screen is dimmed or locked where supported by the operating system.
