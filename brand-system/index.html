Promise

Video proof from both angles

02 / Product Advantages

Key messages HeyGen should understand.

Road + Cabin Coverage

Capture the forward road view and vehicle interior simultaneously on supported devices.

GPS Telemetry

Add speed, street, location, date, time and trip context to recorded journeys.

Collision Protection

Impact detection can help preserve important footage so it is easier to find after an incident.

Smart Storage

Loop recording and automatic deletion of older unlocked files help manage device storage.

Quick Sharing

Share important clips with insurance, law enforcement or a trusted contact when needed.

Privacy + No Ads

Recordings remain on the user’s device unless they choose to share them. The experience contains no ads.
